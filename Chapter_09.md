## 9.1. Purpose & Open Falsification Philosophy
In accordance with the monograph's core philosophy (*"This is Shared Work"*), Chapters 1–8 represent our best validated baseline architecture—not an immutable dogma. If empirical bench testing, industrial manufacturing practice, or cross-disciplinary engineering demonstrates a simpler, cheaper, more durable, or higher-efficiency alternative for any node in the "3-in-1" stack, **the baseline component is upgraded without hesitation**.

Chapter 9 serves as the **official Request-for-Comments (RFC) Sandbox**: an open, structured incubator where any contributor can propose hardware substitutions, alternative materials, novel biological consortia, external technology integrations, or unforeseen deployment environments.

## 9.2. Comparative Benchmark of Open-Standard Evolution Models

| Governance Approach | Core Advantage | Why It Underperforms for Hardware & Science |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Closed Academic Monographs** | High initial editorial polish. | Becomes obsolete within 2–3 years; outsiders cannot submit empirical corrections or field adaptations. |
| **Unmoderated Wiki Pages** | Anyone can edit text instantly. | Vulnerable to pseudoscience, unverified claims, and accidental destruction of thermodynamic balances. |
| **Structured RFC Pipeline (Chapter 9)** | Combines open community innovation with strict engineering review. | Separates experimental proposals from the core specification until bench-verified, then promotes winners with full DOI co-authorship. |

## 9.3. Official RFC Submission Template (Copy & Post in Discussions)
To submit a formal modification or extension proposal, open a new thread in **`Chapter 9: RFC Sandbox (New Proposals)`** inside GitHub Discussions using the following four-part structure:

```text
### [RFC-XXX] Short Title of Proposed Modification or Integration
* **Proposer(s) & Affiliation / Contact:** (Name, Lab/Factory/Independent, GitHub handle)
* **Target Chapter / Module Affected:** (e.g., Chapter 3 Macrocycles / Chapter 5 Heat Loop / New External Integration)
* **1. Problem or Bottleneck Identified:** (What limitation, cost barrier, or efficiency loss in the current baseline does this address?)
* **2. Proposed Technical Change:** (Exact material substitution, geometry change, chemical formula, or external system interface.)
* **3. Expected Quantitative Gain & Trade-Offs:** (Estimated impact on efficiency, CAPEX/OPEX, lifespan, or scalability—plus any potential downsides.)
* **4. Verification Protocol (How to Falsify or Prove):** (Specific bench test, simulation, or pilot metric required to validate the proposal.)
```

## 9.4. Lifecycle of an RFC Proposal (From Idea to Core Standard)
1. **Stage 1 — Community Triage (GitHub Discussions):** Section Moderators verify formatting, ensure compliance with the **BSL-1 Biosafety & Peaceful Use Mandate**, and open peer debate.
2. **Stage 2 — Domain Co-Curator & Architect Audit:** Relevant Domain Co-Curators (Science / Manufacturing) and the Lead System Architect evaluate thermodynamic and economic feasibility. If physical bench testing is required, the Architect drafts a Milestone Scope for funding review by the **Oversight & Treasury Board**.
3. **Stage 3 — Empirical Validation & Promotion:** Once validated by open empirical data (or unrefuted prior literature), the RFC is **promoted into Chapters 1–8** in the next versioned Zenodo release, and the proposer is formally added as an **Official Co-Author** in the DOI metadata.

## 9.5. Priority Open RFC Challenges (Active Calls for Proposals)
* **RFC-001 (Atmospheric Water Harvesting Integration):** Coupling hygroscopic MOF/hydrogel nocturnal moisture harvesters with the second-loop thermal regeneration cycle for hyper-arid inland deserts lacking brackish groundwater.
* **RFC-002 (Municipal & Agro-Industrial Wastewater Valorization):** Adapting the dark bioreactor and thermal pasteurization loop to purify nutrient-rich wastewater while harvesting protein biomass and clean irrigation water.
* **RFC-003 (Offshore & Coastal Floating "3-in-1" Platforms):** Engineering salt-spray-resistant modular floating arrays for marine desalination, seaweed/microalgae biofuel synthesis, and coastal carbon sequestration.
* **RFC-004 (Sub-Zero & Lunar/Martian Regolith Adaptation):** Tailoring freeze-tolerant heat-transfer fluids and closed-loop mineral recycling for polar research stations and extraterrestrial habitats.
